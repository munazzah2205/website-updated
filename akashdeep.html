PROJECT
OVERVIEW

Akashdeep Design Studio wanted to transform their outdated website into a modern and visually distinctive digital presence. As the brand enters a new era under the next generation, the website needed to showcase their architectural expertise while effectively renewing brand identity rooted in heritage, innovation and contemporary design.

We aimed to create a multi-experience platform with unique layout structures, animation-driven storytelling, and professional-driven interactions—all aligned with the client’s vision of rebranding into a competitive design industry.

CLIENT
CONTEXT

Akashdeep Design Studio is an established architecture and interior design practice known for delivering modern, innovative, and functionally-driven spaces. With the next generation taking charge, the studio is expanding into new markets and requires a digital presence that reflects their long-standing legacy and forward-thinking approach.

KEY INTERFACES

THE PORTAL
(HOME)

Design Considerations

A minimal hero ensures instant clarity, supported by intuitive navigation and subtle motion, while varied layouts and a consistent visual language maintain engagement and cohesive brand identity.

Psychological Rationale

The CTA is positioned at the end to align with user commitment readiness, encouraging action only after trust is built through content, while soft color treatment reduces pressure and supports natural decision-making.

THE EVOLUTION
(ABOUT)

Design Considerations

A cohesive tone balances heritage and modernity, with curated imagery and progressive content reflecting the studio’s evolution. A horizontal layout, inline profiles, and restrained motion ensure seamless, uninterrupted exploration.

Cognitive Load Consideration

“Our Team” and “Team Gallery” are kept simple to reduce cognitive load, maintain focus on people, and conclude the page on a clear, human note.

THE ARCHIVE
(WORK)

Design Considerations

A focused, intuitive portfolio experience that balances visual impact with structure, reinforcing credibility while making project exploration effortless.

Interaction & Information Efficiency

Bold typography conveys scale and confidence, while grouping projects by service type reduces search effort. Hover states offer quick previews, enabling faster and more informed selection.

THE DETAIL
(INSIDER)

Design Considerations

Breadcrumbs reinforce hierarchy and enable quick navigation. A minimal hero pairs strong imagery with the project name for instant recognition. Structured metadata and grouped content reduce cognitive load, while clear sections convey depth without overwhelming.

Navigation Flow Optimization

Encourages continued exploration without requiring a return to the main listing. Inline project suggestions enable seamless browsing and keep users engaged within the portfolio ecosystem.

ANIMATION APPROACH

Controlled and purposeful motion to maintain clarity while adding a layer of sophisticated interaction. The motion reflects the 'Architectural Monolith' aesthetic: heavy, deliberate, and structural.

▶

SUBTLE INTERACTION TRANSITIONS

Image zoom & responsive layout shifts

Subtle image zoom and layout shifts on hover enhance depth without disrupting clarity.

▶

HOVER TRANSITION

Depth-driven image expansion

Project cards respond to user presence with subtle scale and shadow shifts, emphasizing structural depth.

▶

MAGNETIC MICRO-INTERACTION

Tactile cursor response

UI elements exert a subtle pull on the cursor, creating a tactile connection between user and interface.

RESPONSIVE ADAPTATION

A desktop-first layout system was used, with responsiveness handled through flexible grids and adaptive stacking across breakpoints. Select sections were refined to preserve hierarchy and clarity across smaller screens.

LEARNINGS

02 / USER ENGAGEMENT
